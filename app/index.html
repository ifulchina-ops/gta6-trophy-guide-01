    Roadmap to the platinum

    Play in this order. Story first — it unlocks the map, the contacts and the activities the other trophies need — then the strands, then the collectibles, then the long counts. The platinum pops the moment the 54th other trophy does.

      Built from the official trophy descriptions. Times are honest estimates for a first playthrough: the story is the bulk of the run, the counts are the tail.

      Missable trophies

      Five trophies need the right moment. Everything else can be earned after the story — missions replay, collectibles stay in the world, and counts are cumulative.

        Partner trophies (Hot Together, Retail Therapy) need your partner beside you in free roam — do them before the final mission, because who is available afterwards is not confirmed. The rest are one-chance opportunities inside a strand or a challenge.
