    Roadmap to the platinum

    Play in this order. The story first — it opens the map, the contacts and the activities the other trophies hang off — then the stranger strands while their markers are up, then the skill checks, then the robberies, then one long map sweep, then the money and the counters. The times are first-playthrough estimates; the story is two thirds of the run and the counts are the tail.

        Each phase lists the chapters and missions it covers, the trophies it earns and how many of them are already ticked. The platinum pops the moment the other 54 are earned — there is no separate task behind it.

        Missables & finale prep

        Five trophies need the right moment. Everything else waits for you: missions replay, collectibles stay in the world, and every count is cumulative.

        Do the partner trophies before the final mission. Hot Together and Retail Therapy both need your partner beside you in free roam, and no official source says who is still available after the ending. Treat the checklist below as your pre-finale save point.

          Before the finale — do these first

          Tick each one as you finish it. The ticks are saved in this browser beside your trophy progress, so they survive a reload.

            The other missables are one-chance moments inside a strand: take Cal's fishing challenge the moment he offers it, never shoot Bret's unique animal before his hunt begins, and treat a spotted jewelry robbery as failed and start it again clean.

            Sources & method

            Every name, description and grade below is the official PlayStation 5 list as Exophase and PSNProfiles publish it. Every guide is written for this atlas; sources are cited, never copied.

            What CONFIRMED means
            The requirement is spelled out in the official trophy description itself — the mission name, the chapter, or a count. Those guides only explain how to reach the thing the description already names.
            What EXPECTED means
            The description names the goal but not the route, so the method is reasoned from it plus GTA V and GTA Online precedent and what the two GTA VI trailers show. The list was published before launch, so nothing here has been played and checked in the retail game. Those guides say so.

              How your progress is kept

              Ticks live in this browser's localStorage and are never sent anywhere. Export writes them to a JSON file you keep; import reads one back, which is how you move progress between browsers; reset clears everything after a confirmation. Nothing in this app makes a network request.
